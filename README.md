# Campus Board 📌

AI-powered personalized campus information platform.

Built for the SheVibes Vibeathon 2026.

## Problem

College students receive important information through multiple scattered channels such as class groups, department announcements, societies, emails and notice boards.

The result is information overload, missed deadlines and difficulty knowing what actually matters.

## Solution

Campus Board uses AI to transform scattered announcements into clear, relevant and actionable information.

Students receive a personalized campus scrapbook that helps them understand:

- What matters to them
- Why it matters
- What they need to do
- When they need to do it

## Core Features

- Personalized onboarding
- AI announcement summarization
- AI prioritization
- Academic notices
- Campus opportunities
- Personalized campus board
- Calendar and deadlines
- CR/Admin announcement posting
- Source and trust information
- Discovery of opportunities outside user preferences
